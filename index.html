The Blueprint

Alessia
Bosio

I'm a graphic designer and 3D modeler based in Brescia — but my mind is always somewhere between a 3D model and a layout. I studied graphic design at the Santa Giulia Academy of Fine Arts, and from there I decided to venture into another field: I'm currently specializing in 3D Fashion Design at the ITS Academy Macchina Lonati, while interning at Barbieri Creazioni S.p.A., an Italian jewelry and fashion accessories company.

My favorite place? That strange corner where editorial design meets the technical precision of 3D. Photo editing for catalogs, jewelry models built curve by curve in Rhinoceros 8, digital clothing sewn in CLO3D — every project is a different puzzle, and I can't leave a single piece out of place. Because every shadow in an image, every node in a mesh, every typographic choice tells a story. And I want you to feel it.
