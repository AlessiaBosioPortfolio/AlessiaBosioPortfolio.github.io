7 Pieces — Lisci & Zirconati
01 / CONCEPT PHASE

The Synthesising Anatomy

A curated piercing collection born from the intersection of botanical precision and contemporary luxury jewelry design. Seven pieces spanning two lines — Lisci (minimal geometric) and Zirconati (multi-stone) — each rendered in both gold and 925 silver. Modeled curve by curve in Rhinoceros 8 with iJewels rendering.

Engine Parameters
Rhinoceros 8 iJewels Render Free Jewels 7 Pieces 2 Colorways

Mathematical Execution

Every element was modeled curve by curve in Rhinoceros 8. Gem settings were calculated using the Free Jewels ecosystem to ensure perfect physical tolerances. Each piece was rendered in photorealistic dual colorways — yellow gold and 925 silver — blurring the line between digital artifact and tangible precious object.

High-Fidelity Cloth Displacement
05 / TEXTURE ARCHITECTURE

Monochromatic Depth

A monochromatic academic and conceptual exercise immersed in shades of deep forest green. A careful three-dimensional analysis of the behavior of complex textile volumes: the chunky cable-knit sweater contrasts sharply with the geometric stability of the cotton cargo shorts.

Photoshop Displacement Map Pipeline

The textile texture was generated by isolating macro patterns from high-resolution images, cleaned up, and processed with masks and tonal values ​​in Photoshop to obtain hyper-realistic specular and normal maps. The channels were then applied to the CLO3D physics engine for flawless rendering of the material.

Barbieri Creazioni S.p.A.

Esperienza di stage presso Barbieri Creazioni S.p.A., azienda italiana specializzata in gioielleria e accessori moda. Il lavoro copre l'intero spettro della comunicazione visiva del brand: dal materiale punto vendita (crowner) alla stampa editoriale (flyer, catalogo), alla documentazione tecnica (schede tecniche) e alla post-produzione fotografica.

Materiale promozionale sviluppato per una campagna stagionale o lancio prodotto. Il layout bilancia l'identità di brand con una gerarchia comunicativa chiara e immediata.

I am currently specializing in 3D Fashion Design at the ITS Academy Macchina Lonati, while simultaneously integrating professional field practice through an internship at Barbieri Creazioni S.p.A., an Italian company in the jewelry and fashion accessories sector.
