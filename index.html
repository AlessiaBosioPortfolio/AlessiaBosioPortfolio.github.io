Fashion 3D · Texture Study

Midori

2025
CLO3D, Photoshop
Digital Fashion

A monochromatic fashion study immersed in deep forest green — a meditation on texture, volume, and surface. The chunky cable-knit sweater in contrast with smooth cargo shorts demonstrates the power of CLO3D in simulating fabric behaviour with precision: from the irregular mesh of the knitwear to the crisp lines of the cotton shorts. Texture created starting from an image and worked with Photoshop to obtain the maximum realism of the material and then inserted into CLO3D to have the perfect render.

The Blueprint

Alessia
Bosio

I'm a graphic designer and 3D modeler based in Brescia—but my mind is always somewhere between a 3D model and a layout. I studied graphic design at the Santa Giulia Academy of Fine Arts, and from there I decided to venture into another field: I'm currently specializing in 3D Fashion Design at the ITS Academy Macchina Lonati, while I'm interning at Barbieri Creazioni S.p.A., an Italian jewelry and fashion accessories company.

My favorite place? That strange corner where editorial design meets the technical precision of 3D. Photo editing for catalogs, jewelry models built curve by curve in Rhinoceros 8, digital clothing sewn in CLO3D—every project is a different puzzle, and I can't leave a single piece out of place. I'm obsessed with detail—in the best sense of the word. Because every shadow in an image, every node in a mesh, every typographic choice is never random: it tells a story. And I want you to feel it.
