01 / CONCEPT PHASE

The Synthesising Anatomy

A curated piercing collection born from the intersection of botanical precision and contemporary luxury jewelry design. Seven pieces spanning two lines — Lisci (minimal geometric) and Zirconati (multi-stone) — each rendered in both gold and 925 silver. Modeled curve by curve in Rhinoceros 8 with iJewels rendering.

Engine Parameters
Rhinoceros 8 iJewels Render Free Jewels 7 Pieces 2 Colorways

Mathematical Execution

Every element was modeled curve by curve in Rhinoceros 8. Gem settings were calculated using the Free Jewels ecosystem to ensure perfect physical tolerances. Each piece was rendered in photorealistic dual colorways — yellow gold and 925 silver — blurring the line between digital artifact and tangible precious object. 05 / TEXTURE ARCHITECTURE

Monochromatic Depth

A monochromatic academic and conceptual exercise immersed in shades of deep forest green. A careful three-dimensional analysis of the behavior of complex textile volumes: the chunky cable-knit sweater contrasts sharply with the geometric stability of the cotton cargo shorts.

Photoshop Displacement Map Pipeline

The textile texture was generated by isolating macro patterns from high-resolution images, cleaned up, and processed with masks and tonal values ​​in Photoshop to obtain hyper-realistic specular and normal maps. The channels were then applied to the CLO3D physics engine for flawless rendering of the material.

3D Fashion Design · CLO3D Simulation

Guess Kids
Back to School

2025
CLO3D
ITS Academy × GUESS Collab
Kids 8–16yo
07 / DIGITAL FASHION COLLECTION

Class of 2026

A digital fashion project developed in collaboration between ITS Academy Macchina Lonati and GUESS. Under the creative direction of Marco Terzi, Head Designer of GUESS Man & Kids, the challenge was to design a complete Back to School collection for the 8–16 age range — two full outfits per gender, one casual, one sportswear — entirely constructed and simulated in CLO3D.

Engine Parameters
CLO3D Fabric Physics Print Design Colorway Studies

Two Directions, One Identity

The collection unfolds along two parallel visual tracks. A soft feminine line draws from school-girl nostalgia — bow-print all-over patterns, black lace trims on sleeve cuffs and waistbands, argyle knit vests over white ribbed tops, and fluid A-line denim skirts in textured pleats. The sportswear track builds entirely around GUESS's signature cobalt blue, with logo embroidery, wave-pattern color blocking, and relaxed silhouettes engineered for active youth lifestyles. Every garment was built in CLO3D from pattern draft to final render, with precise fabric physics, multi-colorway simulations, and print placement studies.

Sportswear Direction

The sportswear line is anchored in bold graphic athleticism. Wide-leg black pants with geometric white side panels, cropped half-zip tops, and the signature GUESS cobalt tracksuit — jogger pants with oversized number print and a cropped sweatshirt with bold logo chest placement — define a clean, high-energy silhouette. The blue varsity jacket and matching wide-leg pants with fluid wave-pattern color blocking are the collection's statement piece, designed for maximum visual impact on the school hallway and beyond.

Casual Direction

The casual line translates a contemporary feminine aesthetic into age-appropriate wearable pieces. A cropped white tee with black lace sleeve trim pairs with wide-leg grey bow-print pants; a white long-sleeve base layers under an argyle diamond-pattern knit vest paired with a textured denim pleated skirt; a red turtleneck sweatshirt with all-over bow print sits above a clean heathered mini skirt. The bow motif appears in multiple forms — as all-over print on jersey and as a vertical decorative column on dark denim wide-legs — creating a cohesive signature detail across the entire direction.

Colorway & Fabric Physics

Each garment was developed with multiple colorway explorations rendered directly in CLO3D, testing how fabric weight and drape changed across different textile presets. Sleeve construction details — cuffs, ribbing, lace trims — were studied in isolation to verify fit and proportion at the digital fitting stage, before assembling the complete styled looks.
